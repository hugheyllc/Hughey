# The Law Firm Marketing Operating System

A marketing operating system is the framework that connects your strategy, tactics, and measurement. Without one, marketing becomes reactive.

## The Core Components

1. **Strategy** — Your annual positioning and growth goals
2. **Channels** — The specific platforms where your clients exist
3. **Execution** — The weekly and monthly campaigns
4. **Measurement** — Real data on what's working
5. **Iteration** — The monthly/quarterly reviews

Most law firms have pieces of this but they don't talk to each other.
